It started with a small thing.
A question that didn’t feel dangerous at the time.

But questions are like loose threads—pull one, and suddenly the whole sweater is on the floor and you’re cold and wondering when that happened.

Down the rabbit hole we go.

At first, it’s interesting. Harmless. You tell yourself you’ll stop soon. One more click. One more thought. One more what if. The walls begin to curve, logic bends politely out of the way, and time starts behaving like a suggestion instead of a rule.

Things repeat, but not exactly the same.
Familiar, but tilted.
Like déjà vu wearing a disguise.

You pass ideas arguing with each other. Facts that don’t agree on being facts. Certainty dissolves into possibility, and possibility multiplies when you’re not looking. Gravity feels optional. So does doubt. So does you.

The deeper you go, the quieter the surface becomes. No notifications. No clocks. Just layers upon layers of why stacked like mirrors facing mirrors.

You realize too late that the rabbit hole isn’t pulling you down.

You’re leaning in.

And somewhere below—
or maybe above—
something is smiling, waiting to see what you become once you stop trying to climb back out.
